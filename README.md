# From Scrolling to Shopping

### How Social Media Is Changing Consumer Buying Behavior

Author:  Likhita Sambhana

---

## 📌 About This Article

Social media has transformed the way consumers discover, evaluate, and purchase products.

This article explores how platforms such as Instagram influence consumer buying behavior through visual content, influencers, social proof, recommendations, and personalized experiences.

The article also reflects on real-life examples of how social media exposure can influence everyday purchasing decisions.

## 🎯 Marketing Concepts Covered

* Consumer Buying Behavior
* Social Media Marketing
* Influencer Marketing
* Digital Consumer Psychology
* Social Proof
* Impulse Buying
* Brand Awareness
* Content Marketing
* Purchase Decisions

## 💡 Key Insight

Consumers are no longer influenced only by traditional advertising.

Today, a product can move from **being unknown → being discovered → being desired → being purchased** through a single social media interaction.
